MS-LANet: Multi-Scale Local-Attention Network

Designed MS-LANet to overcome sequence dependency bottlenecks in 10-class protein subcellular localization (N=13,907) by fusing multi-scale 1D CNNs with self-attention mechanisms.

Acc: 84.82% MCC: 0.8120 +6.87% over baselines

AMP-MHSA: Transformer for Antimicrobial Peptide Discovery

Developed AMP-MHSA to resolve biophysical feature loss in AMP classification (N=8,000) by constructing a Transformer fusing helical positional encodings, Wimley-White transfer energy, and ESM-2 650M embeddings.

Acc: 93.40% MCC: 0.8687 +8.40% over SOTA
Transformers ESM-2 Helical PE Biophysics
GitHub
⚗️

PROTAC-Degrade3D: 3D Equivariant GNN Engine

Proposed PROTAC-Degrade3D to address 3D spatial ubiquitination transfer bottlenecks in targeted protein degradation (N=2,832) by coupling 3D-EGNNs with a novel Ubiquitination Geometry Module (UGM).

Acc: 97.46% MCC: 0.9444 +19.51% over DeepPROTACs
